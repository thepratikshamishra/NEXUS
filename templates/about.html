ABOUT THE PROJECT

NEXUS: learning with direction.

A student-focused web application prototype for career-oriented skill planning.

N
PROJECT PURPOSE

Bring your goal and your next actions into one place.

NEXUS is designed to help students choose a target role, record their current skills, compare those skills with a local role checklist, maintain learning tasks, record study time, and explore hypothetical study plans.

01 / PROFILE

Personal context

Keep a target role, current skills, and weekly study availability.

02 / ANALYSIS

Transparent skill matching

Compare skill names with a simple role dataset. This is rule-based, not AI prediction.

03 / PLANNING

Action and reflection

Track roadmap tasks, study sessions, and possible time allocations.
